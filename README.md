### Description of the dataset

The dataset consists of information from 244 restaurant bills, collected in the US in 1987. It includes details about the tips given to restaurant staff, such as the total bill, tip amount, gender of the person paying, smoking status, day of the week, time of day, and party size.

The data table used in the project is named **'tips'**. Description of the columns in the **'tips'** table:

- **id**: identifier for each bill record.
- **total_bill**: total amount of invoice the customer has to pay.
- **tip**: tip amount paid by the customer.
- **sex**: gender of the person paying.
- **smoker**: smoking status (Yes/No).
- **day**: day of the week.
- **time**: time of day.
- **size**: party size.
